# Questions

The agents write a card here when they need a decision from you. Answer under the card, then tell the agent in its pane: "I answered your card."

Card format:

```text
## Q1: <the question>
From: builder | reviewer
Stakes: what happens if we get this wrong
Options: A) ...  B) ...
Recommendation: A, because ...
What I checked: ...
Answer: (yours)
```
