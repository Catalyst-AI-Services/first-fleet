## The goal

Turn `meeting-notes.txt` into `summary.md`: a one-page summary of the meeting that someone who missed it can act on.

## What done looks like

- `summary.md` has three sections: Decisions, Action items, Open questions.
- Every action item names an owner and a due date, both taken from the notes.
- If the notes don't give an owner or a date, the item says "not set in the notes". Never guess one.
- Nothing appears in the summary that isn't in the notes.
- It fits on one page (under 40 lines).

## Off limits

- Don't change or delete `meeting-notes.txt`.
- Don't create files outside this folder.
